Account & Data Deletion

For the game Don't Blink: Dark Runner by AliTechIT

Don't Blink: Dark Runner does not require you to create an account. The only data tied to you is what you choose to put on the leaderboard. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

What data we store

  • Display name (nickname): the name you typed when saving a score.
  • Best score: your highest score, shown on the leaderboard.
  • Anonymous game ID: a random identifier created for guest play (not tied to your Google account, email, or real name).

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at the address below.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Don't Blink data”.
  3. Tell us your leaderboard nickname (and roughly your score) so we can find your entry.
aekremsan@gmail.com

What gets deleted, and when

  • Deleted: your leaderboard entry (nickname + best score) and the associated anonymous game record.
  • Kept: nothing personal — there is no other personal data stored.
  • Timeframe: requests are completed within 30 days.
