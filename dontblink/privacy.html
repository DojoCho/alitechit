DON'T BLINK · DARK RUNNER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Don't Blink: Dark Runner (package com.alitechit.games.dontblink, the "Game"), published by AliTech IT ("we", "us"), handles information. By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

Short version: we don't ask for your email, password, or real identity. The only data the Game stores is the nickname you choose for the leaderboard and your best score.

Information we collect

  • Display name (nickname): a name you type when you save a score. It is optional — if you skip it, a random name like "Wanderer123" is generated. Please do not enter real personal information as your nickname, since it is shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Best score: your highest score, stored so it can appear on the global leaderboard.
  • Anonymous game ID: when you first play, the Game signs in anonymously and receives a random anonymous identifier so your score can be linked to your device. This is not tied to your Google account, email, or real name.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers. The Game contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How we use the information

  • To display the global high-score leaderboard inside the Game.
  • To remember your best score and chosen nickname between sessions.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information for advertising or marketing.

Service providers

The leaderboard and anonymous sign-in are powered by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The data described above is stored on Google's servers on our behalf. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Network access

The Game requests Internet permission solely to sync scores with the leaderboard. No other network activity takes place.

Data retention & deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept so the rankings remain available. If you would like your nickname and score removed from the leaderboard, email us at the address below with a description of your entry (e.g. your nickname and approximate score) and we will delete it.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the only stored values are a self-chosen nickname and a score, no personal data needs to be provided to play.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
