Privacy Policy — Rust Raid Calculator

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Rust Raid Calculator (package com.alitechit.rustraidcalc) is developed by alitechit.com. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not read your contacts, location, files, camera, microphone or device identifiers, and it requests no runtime permissions.

Data stored on your device

Two things are saved locally so the app remembers them between sessions:

  • your language choice (English or Turkish);
  • the raid plan currently on screen — the targets and explosives you picked.

Both live in the app's own local storage on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network use

The app works entirely offline. All game data, item icons and calculations ship inside the app itself; it makes no network requests during normal use.

Two optional actions involve other apps: tapping Share this raid hands a link to your device's share sheet, and tapping a store badge opens Google Play or the App Store. What happens after that is governed by the app you chose, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects nothing, it cannot collect anything from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.
