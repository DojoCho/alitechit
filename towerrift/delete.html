TowerRift — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 14 September 2026

In the current version there is no account to delete. TowerRift does not yet have sign-in or a server, so everything the game knows about you lives in a file on your own phone and deleting it is entirely in your hands.
Accounts are coming. Sign-in with Google Play Games and with Apple, plus cloud saves, are planned, and an Apple App Store release follows the Google Play one. When accounts exist you will be able to request deletion of the server-side copy from this page, and the request address below already works for that.

Delete everything

Either of these removes all game data permanently:

  1. Uninstall the app. Android deletes its private storage with it.
  2. Or clear its storage: Settings → Apps → TowerRift → Storage → Clear data.

Both wipe your progress — chapters, talents, currency and settings. This cannot be undone, and there is no backup anywhere to restore from.

What about purchases?

Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by us. Deleting game data does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel a monthly pass from the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.
