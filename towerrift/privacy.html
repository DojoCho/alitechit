Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Controller: Ali Ekrem San (AliTechIT) · info@alitechit.com

TowerRift records how the game is played, not who you are. Everything collected is there to tune the balance and to give you your account back if you lose your phone. There are no ad networks, no data sales and no third-party tracking.

1. What is collected

1.1 Your account

DataWhyWhere from
Account id (a UUID the game generates)The account itself Made on the device

Sign in with Apple is asked for neither your name nor your e-mail address. The game uses neither, and a permission that will not be used is a permission that needs explaining.

If you link Google, your e-mail address and profile picture are not kept. Google's identity token carries them; the server deletes both immediately after linking. What remains is the identifier that lets us recognise you.

1.2 Gameplay statistics

When a run finishes, this is recorded:

  • which mode and which chapter
  • the wave it ended on, and whether it was cleared
  • core health after each wave
  • how many towers were built and the highest level reached
  • gold held and gold spent on talents
  • how many enemies leaked
  • the build version

Not sent: your name, your tag, your messages, your friends list, your location, your device model, any advertising identifier, or a record of your IP address. The row carries no identifier of its own — the server already knows the session the request came from.

You are asked on first launch and nothing is recorded until you answer. No is an answer too: the question is not asked again. You can change your mind later under Settings → Data → Gameplay statistics. Turning it off stops new rows and deletes the ones waiting on your phone.

1.3 What is never collected

Advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone, device model or operating system version, browsing history, and payment details — purchases go through Google Play or the App Store and card information never reaches us.

2. How long it is kept

DataRetention
Account and progressUntil the account is deleted
Gameplay statistics — raw row on the server 60 days
Gameplay statistics — daily summary Indefinitely, with no identifier: day, chapter, numbers
Gameplay statistics — local archive Indefinitely, with no identifier

When a raw row is deleted, the only link between it and an account goes with it. The summary that remains does not say who you are.

The local archive. Raw rows are copied to the developer's own machine before the server deletes them, because a deleted row is a question that can never be asked again. The account id does not travel with them: the copied row says what happened in a run, not who played it. A row in the archive therefore cannot be tied to a player, and a deletion request leaves nothing there to delete.

3. Where it is stored

The server runs on Oracle Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany, using Nakama and PostgreSQL. Connections are over HTTPS. The admin console is closed to the internet and reachable only through an SSH tunnel.

4. Who it is shared with

Nobody. There is no ad network, no analytics SDK and no data broker. Your data is not sold. The one exception would be a legal obligation.

The game contains no third-party component that sends data anywhere other than the game's own server. Signing in with Google authenticates you to Google; it tells Google nothing about the game.

5. Your rights

  • See it. You can ask what is held on your account.
  • Delete it. Settings → Account → Delete my account, inside the game, with no waiting: it destroys the account on the server, takes the arena record and the raw gameplay rows with it, and cannot be undone. See the account deletion page.
  • Stop it. Statistics can be turned off in settings without affecting your account.
  • Take it with you. You can ask for a copy of your save.

For seeing, stopping and porting, write to info@alitechit.com. Including your tag (#XXXXXXXX, under Settings → Account) makes the account easy to find. If you can no longer reach the game — the phone is gone, the app is uninstalled — a deletion request can be made by e-mail and is completed within 30 days.

6. Children

TowerRift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If such an account comes to our attention it is deleted.

7. Changes

If this text changes, its date changes with it, and anything significant is also posted to the mailbox inside the game.

1.2 Oyun istatistikleri

Bir koşu bittiğinde şunlar kaydedilir:

  • hangi mod ve hangi bölüm
  • kaçıncı dalgada bitti, geçildi mi
  • her dalgadan sonra çekirdekte kalan can
  • kaç kule kuruldu, en yükseği kaçıncı seviye
  • hesaptaki altın ve yeteneklere harcanmış altın
  • kaç düşman sızdı
  • oyunun sürümü

Gönderilmeyenler: adın, etiketin, mesajların, arkadaş listen, konumun, cihaz modelin, reklam kimliğin, IP'nin kaydı. Satırın içinde ayrıca bir kimlik göndermiyoruz — sunucu isteği zaten senin oturumundan tanıyor.

İlk açılışta soruluyor ve cevaplanana kadar hiçbir şey kaydedilmiyor. Hayır demek de bir cevaptır: soru bir daha sorulmaz. Kararını sonra Ayarlar → Veri → Oyun istatistikleri altından değiştirebilirsin. Kapattığında yeni kayıt oluşmaz ve telefonunda bekleyenler silinir.

1.3 Hiç toplamadıklarımız

Reklam kimliği, konum, rehber, fotoğraflar, mikrofon, cihaz modeli veya işletim sistemi sürümü, tarayıcı geçmişi ve ödeme bilgisi — satın alma Google Play ya da App Store üzerinden geçer, kart bilgisi bize hiç ulaşmaz.

3. Nerede duruyor

Sunucu Oracle Cloud'da, Almanya/Frankfurt bölgesinde; Nakama ve PostgreSQL. Bağlantı HTTPS. Yönetim paneli internete kapalı, yalnızca SSH tüneliyle açılıyor.

4. Kimlerle paylaşıyoruz

Hiç kimseyle. Reklam ağı, analitik SDK'sı, veri simsarı yok. Verini satmıyoruz. Tek istisna yasal bir zorunluluk doğması olurdu.

Oyunun kendi sunucusu dışında veri gönderen hiçbir üçüncü taraf bileşeni yok. Google ile giriş, Google'ın kimlik doğrulaması içindir ve Google'a oyun hakkında bir şey söylemez.

5. Hakların

  • Silmek: Ayarlar → Hesap → Hesabımı sil. Oyunun içinden, beklemeden: hesabı sunucuda yok eder, arena kaydını ve ham oyun istatistiklerini de alır, ve geri dönüşü yoktur. Hesap silme sayfasına bak.
  • Durdurmak: istatistikleri ayarlardan kapatabilirsin, hesabın etkilenmez.
  • Taşımak: kaydının bir kopyasını isteyebilirsin.

Görmek, durdurmak ve taşımak için: info@alitechit.com. Etiketini (#XXXXXXXX, Ayarlar → Hesap) yazarsan hesabını bulmak kolay olur. Oyuna artık ulaşamıyorsan — telefon gitti, uygulama silindi — silme talebi e-postayla da yapılabilir ve 30 gün içinde yerine getirilir.

6. Çocuklar

TowerRift 13 yaşın altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir ve bilerek onlardan veri toplamaz. Böyle bir hesabın farkına varırsak sileriz.
