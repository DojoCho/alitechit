TowerRift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This version of TowerRift does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing from your device to us or to anyone else.
What is coming. Sign-in with Google Play Games and with Apple, cloud saves and online play are all planned, and the Apple App Store release is close behind the Google Play one. Those versions will handle account data. This page describes the version you can install today; it will be rewritten, and the change described here, before any version that collects data is released.

What is stored, and where

Your progress — chapters cleared, talents bought, currency, settings — is written to a save file in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the phone. Nobody, including us, can read it remotely.

The game also generates a random identifier for a future cloud-save feature. It is created on your device, stored beside the save file, and is not transmitted anywhere today.

Permissions

  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback during battles. You can turn it off in the in-game settings.
  • Internet — declared for online features that are planned but not yet built. The current version makes no network requests at all.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details are never seen, processed or stored by us. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Signing in

Today there is nothing to sign in to. When sign-in arrives, linking a Google or Apple account will be optional and will exist for one purpose: carrying your progress between devices and between Android and iOS. The plan is to store the account identifier the platform gives us and your game progress — not your name, email, contacts or friends list. When it ships, this section will say exactly what is stored rather than what is planned.

Children

TowerRift is rated for ages 12 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

The game ships with no third-party SDKs — no ad networks, no analytics providers, no social integrations. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Deleting your data

All data is local, so uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android Settings, removes everything. See the account and data deletion page for details.

Changes to this policy

Online play and cloud saves are planned. If a future version starts sending data, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than added quietly.
